    MONOGRAMS

    Can I use it as a tattoo? What size works best?

    Yes, that’s the most popular use. I recommend at least 3.5 cm. Cut a 3.5 × 3.5 cm square of paper and place it on your skin to check the scale. I create the artwork, and your tattoo artist applies it.

    Can I add a date or number?

    Yes. Regular numerals work well. Roman numerals can blend in with letters and make the symbol harder to read.

    What’s the difference between a monogram and a visual identity?

    A monogram is a symbol based on letters, names or initials. A visual identity includes the symbol, colors, typography and the pieces where they’re used.

    VISUAL IDENTITY

    What are branded pieces?

    These are items that carry the design, such as an invitation, menu, business card or storefront sign. The first four are included. Additional pieces are priced individually in the list.

    Can I choose the pieces later?

    Yes. Send the quote with the pieces you’ve chosen and decide on the rest with me through WhatsApp. You can also swap a piece before I start.

    Is printing included?

    I deliver print-ready PDFs with bleed and crop marks for your preferred printer, plus PNG and JPG files for digital use. Printing is handled by your printer.

    Will I own the design? Can I register it?

    Yes. After final payment, you can use your design and apply for trademark registration with Brazil’s INPI. You or your lawyer handle registration. I provide the files you need.
